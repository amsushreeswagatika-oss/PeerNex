PEERNEX is an AI powered student-to-student platform where students can share their skills, offer services, learn from peers, and find help for their needs. 
Students can offer skills like coding, teaching, designing, video ecditing, poster making and many more.
AI matches students based on their needs and skills, creating a community where students learn , help , collaborate with each other.
There are solutions to problems that many students face lately. We have Student Skill Profiles ,  Safe Student Community , Peer-to-Peer Collaboration , Easy Service Requests , AI-Powered Matching.
Target Users like College and University Students, Students seeking learning support , Creative students , Student Project teams , Student Freelancers.
KEY FEATURES used are AI SKILLMATCH , SKILL EXCHANGE ,TEAM FORMATION , SMART RECOMMENDATIONS , DIGITAL SKILL PASSPORT , RATINGS , SAFETY TRUST.
Future Scopes are expand to more colleges , advanced AI matching , industry connections , mobile app , global student Network.
PEERNEX connects students with the right peers based on their skills , interests , and requirements. It encourages collaboration, skill exchange, practical learning. In the future , PEERNEX can expand to colleges , companies , internships and global student networks.
